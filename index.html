(01) — About

Small team energy, one person deep.

I work at the overlap of design and engineering — sketching the idea, shaping the interface, and shipping it to a real URL. No handoff gaps.

Most of my projects start with a conversation about what a page actually needs to do — sell a product, collect sign-ups, explain something complicated — and work backwards from there to type, layout and code.

I like working with early-stage founders, local businesses and creative people who want a site that feels considered, loads quickly, and is easy to update once I've handed it over.

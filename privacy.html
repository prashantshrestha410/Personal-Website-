Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit or contact Prashant Shrestha through this portfolio website and how that information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a message through the contact form, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Project type, if provided
  • Your message
  • Submission date and time

Email verification may also be used to confirm that you have access to the email address you provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information submitted through the contact form may be used to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and messages.
  • Understand the project or service you are asking about.
  • Receive and manage contact form submissions.
  • Help prevent spam and misuse of the contact form.

3. Third-Party Services

This website uses third-party services to support its contact functionality:

  • Supabase: Used for email verification, backend processing, and storing contact form submissions.
  • Resend: Used to deliver email notifications about submitted contact messages.

These providers process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Contact form submissions may be stored in the website's database. Information is retained as needed for communication, record management, and security purposes. There is currently no fixed public retention period.

5. Data Sharing

Personal information submitted through the contact form is not intended to be sold or rented. It may be processed by the service providers described in this policy or disclosed where required by applicable law.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect submitted information. However, no method of online transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Requests

If you have questions about your personal information or wish to request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, please contact the website owner through the portfolio homepage.

Requests will be reviewed and handled where reasonably possible and as required by applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to its portfolio and contact functionality. If you believe a child has submitted personal information unnecessarily, please contact the website owner.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please use the contact form on the portfolio homepage.
